# Introduction to Generative AI on AWS

## Objectives

- Explain deep learning and generative AI.
- Name AWS generative AI offerings.

## Notes

### Deep learning

- ML subset with decades-old theory that needed modern hardware; neural networks pass summaries through layers of artificial neurons (math functions) to form a model, cracking harder problems like computer vision and NLP.

### Generative AI

- Deep learning producing original content and ideas (conversations, stories, images, music), powered by foundation models (FMs), huge models pre-trained on massive data.
- One FM adapts to many tasks, while classic ML models do one.
- LLMs are popular text-trained FMs for human language; FMs also make video, images, music.

### AWS options

- AWS tools build and scale custom generative AI.
- Amazon SageMaker JumpStart: FM and prebuilt-solution hub, few-click deploys, customizable with your data.
- Amazon Bedrock: fully managed, a wide pick of top-performing FMs from Amazon and other leading AI firms, private adaptation, no infrastructure to run, one API.
- Amazon Q: assistant tied to company repositories for contextual chat, solutions and actions (Q Business, Q Developer).
- Payoff: fresh customer experiences, productivity, innovation.

## Key takeaways

- Generative AI sits inside deep learning, inside ML, inside AI.
- One FM, trained once on massive data, serves many jobs.
- AWS offers JumpStart, Bedrock and Amazon Q.

## Terms

- **Deep learning**: ML using layered neural networks.
- **Neural network**: Brain-inspired layers of artificial neurons.
- **Generative AI**: Deep learning that creates new content.
- **Foundation model (FM)**: Huge pre-trained, multi-task model.
- **Large language model (LLM)**: Text-trained FM.
- **Amazon Q**: Business-tailored AI assistant.
