# Benefits of the AWS Cloud

## Objectives

- Recognize the six cloud benefits and match a business problem to the right one.

## Notes

### Trade fixed expense for variable expense

- Owning a data center means paying hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars upfront for space, hardware, staff and upkeep, then budgeting around it even when usage is low.
- On AWS the bill follows monthly usage, so you start small, spending matches consumption for more financial flexibility, and billing and budgeting tools help you save.
- The course sometimes says upfront expense instead of fixed expense.
- **Exam guide update** (verified_on 2026-10-09, [Six advantages of cloud computing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/whitepapers/latest/aws-overview/six-advantages-of-cloud-computing.html)): Fixed costs are paid up front for data centers and servers whether or not you use them, while variable costs on AWS follow only what you actually consume.

### Benefit from massive economies of scale

- AWS buys hardware in bulk for its worldwide data centers and passes the lower prices on.
- So startups, not only big enterprises, can afford advanced technology.

### Stop guessing capacity

- On premises you buy hardware for a forecast: plan for 10 million users over three years and get about 500,000, and the excess sits idle, while underestimating means a scramble for servers, degraded service and lost customers.
- On AWS you provision for today and scale with real demand in minutes, not weeks or months, keeping performance optimal without over- or under-provisioning.

### Increase speed and agility

- Teams spin up test environments, try ideas and delete failures so costs stop, shifting time from provisioning and deprovisioning to innovating and optimizing.
- Faster deployment shortens time to market and quickens responses to change.

### Stop spending money running and maintaining data centers

- A data center keeps costing money after it is built, for utilities, maintenance, and racking, stacking and powering servers.
- AWS runs that physical layer, freeing time and money for customers and strategy.
- The timeline words this benefit with "to run and maintain" instead.

### Go global in minutes

- A US company expanding to India can deploy to an AWS-managed [Region](04-introduction-to-aws-global-infrastructure.md) there instead of building its own data centers, in minutes rather than months or years.
- **Exam guide update** (verified_on 2026-10-09, [Six advantages of cloud computing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/whitepapers/latest/aws-overview/six-advantages-of-cloud-computing.html)): The exam guide calls this benefit speed of deployment: with go global in minutes, you deploy an application to multiple Regions around the world with just a few clicks.

## Key takeaways

- The six benefits by their course names: trade fixed expense for variable expense, benefit from massive economies of scale, stop guessing capacity, increase speed and agility, stop spending money running and maintaining data centers, and go global in minutes.
- Together they save money and time and open up AWS's worldwide footprint.
- On AWS, spending follows usage and capacity follows demand.

## Scenarios

- Hardware for 10 million users, about 500,000 arrive: stop guessing capacity.
- Throwaway test environments for new ideas: increase speed and agility.
- A US company deploying to a Region in India: go global in minutes.

## Terms

- **Fixed expense**: a cost that does not change with usage.
- **Variable expense**: a cost that follows consumption, like an AWS bill.
- **Economies of scale**: lower unit prices from huge purchase volumes.
- **Scaling**: adjusting resources up or down to demand.
