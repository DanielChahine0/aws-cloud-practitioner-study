# Introduction to Monitoring, Compliance, and Governance in the AWS Cloud

## Objectives

- Order the steps from securing to compliance.

## Notes

### Basis

- Monitoring, audits, compliance and governance build on security.
- Good monitoring reveals usage and catches issues early.

### Four steps

1. **Secure**: stop unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, change or destruction (firewalls, authentication, identity management, vaults).
2. **Monitor**: scan security events, traffic and activity nonstop for threats or anomalies.
3. **Audit**: check periodically that controls work and policies are followed.
4. **Comply**: prove practices meet regulations, standards and contracts.

## Visuals

- Hero: course map, monitoring done.
- Overview: lock, magnifier, checklist and certificate, one per step.
- Timeline: an image per step, such as the security tools above.

## Key takeaways

- Order: secure, monitor, audit, comply.
- Monitoring is constant, audits periodic.
- Compliance covers laws, standards, contracts and internal policies.

## Terms

- **Auditing**: periodic review that controls work.
- **Compliance**: meeting applicable rules and policies.
