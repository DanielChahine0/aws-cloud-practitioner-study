# AWS Health

## Objectives

- Describe AWS Health's uses.

## Notes

### Basics

- AWS Health is the main feed of events affecting your resources.
- It covers service events, planned changes and account notices.
- The dashboard adds updates and timely fix advice to manage service health at scale.
- Uses: plan lifecycle events, troubleshoot incidents.

## Visuals

- Hero: a doctor checks a "hot" EC2 instance.

## Key takeaways

- Check it for events hitting your resources.
- Its API needs AWS Premium Support.
- It is no resource catalog.

## Terms

- **AWS Health**: resource health event source.
- **AWS Health Dashboard**: account-specific health view.
- **AWS Health API**: programmatic access.
