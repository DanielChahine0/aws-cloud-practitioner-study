# AWS Cloud Adoption Framework

## Objectives

- Explain why AWS CAF exists and who built it.
- Match the six perspectives to their focus and roles.

## Notes

### Why AWS CAF exists

- Developers, architects and analysts see migration differently, and newcomers may miss a role.
- AWS Professional Services built AWS CAF to merge those views, align everyone, field the right team and make the move quick and smooth.
- It shares AWS experience and best practices, plus tooling that speeds the journey, organizes resources and aligns leaders.
- Payoffs: revenue, productivity and customer experience up, operating costs and risk down, sustainability and transparency up.
- Uses: legacy infrastructure and app moves, process and operations changes, and new business models.
- **Exam guide update** (verified_on 2026-10-09, [AWS Cloud Adoption Framework](https://aws.amazon.com/cloud-adoption-framework/)): AWS CAF ties cloud adoption to four business outcomes: reduced business risk, improved ESG performance, increased revenue and increased operational efficiency.

### The six perspectives

- Business, People and Governance are business capabilities; Platform, Security and Operations are technical.

| Perspective | Focus | Typical roles |
|---|---|---|
| Business | IT serves business needs and IT spending ties to key business results; build the business case, rank cloud initiatives, align goals | Business and finance managers, budget owners, strategy stakeholders |
| People | Organization-wide change management; review structures, roles, skills and gaps to set training, staffing and org priorities | HR, staffing, people managers |
| Governance | Align IT and business strategy for more value and less risk; update skills and processes, measure cloud investments | CIO, program and portfolio managers, enterprise architects, business analysts |
| Platform | Patterns for new cloud solutions and moving on-premises workloads; architectural models, detailed target state | CTO, IT managers, solutions architects |
| Security | Visibility, auditability, control and agility; choose and implement security controls | CISO, IT security managers and analysts |
| Operations | Enable, run, use, operate and recover workloads at the level agreed with business stakeholders; set daily to yearly routines, support business operations, define current procedures plus needed process changes and training | IT operations and support managers |

- Course mapping: a business or finance analyst to Business (Governance also lists business analysts), HR to People, a cloud architect to Platform.

### AWS CAF Action Plan

- Skill and process gaps found per perspective feed the AWS CAF Action Plan, which guides change management.

## Key takeaways

- AWS CAF organizes guidance into six perspectives and also supplies tools to speed migration.
- People owns change management; Platform owns architecture and moving workloads.
- Perspective gaps feed the AWS CAF Action Plan.

## Terms

- **AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF)**: AWS migration guidance in six perspectives, plus tools to speed the move.
- **Perspective**: One AWS CAF area with its own stakeholders.
- **AWS CAF Action Plan**: A plan built from perspective gaps to steer change management.
