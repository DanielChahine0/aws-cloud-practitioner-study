# Introduction to Migration

## Objectives

- Define cloud migration.
- Name the three phases of a large migration.

## Notes

### Cloud migration

- Moving apps, databases and other IT resources from on premises or another cloud to AWS.
- It takes planning, the move and upkeep, often in iterative stages AWS supports.

### The three phases

- **Assess**: gauge readiness, set outcomes, goals and the need to move, then weigh findings into a business case (Migration Evaluator).
- **Mobilize**: ready the organization and resources, write the plan around app dependencies, close readiness gaps and pick strategies (Application Discovery Service, Migration Hub).
- **Migrate and modernize**: architect, migrate and validate each app, alone or with specialists and competency partners.
- Last-phase tools: Application Migration Service, AWS Database Migration Service (AWS DMS), Migration Hub, and DataSync, Transfer Family or the Snow Family for data.

## Visuals

- Three-phase graphic: a numbered marker per phase with its services.

## Key takeaways

- Order: assess, mobilize, migrate and modernize.
- Assess yields the business case; mobilize, the plan.
- Migration is iterative, not one and done.

## Terms

- **Cloud migration**: Moving IT resources from on premises or another cloud to AWS.
- **Assess phase**: Checking readiness and building the business case.
- **Mobilize phase**: Preparing the organization and writing the migration plan.
- **Migrate and modernize phase**: Moving and validating each app.
