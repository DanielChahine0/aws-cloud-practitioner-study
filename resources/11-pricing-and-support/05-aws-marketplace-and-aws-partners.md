# AWS Marketplace and AWS Partners

## Objectives

- Name Marketplace's main offerings and benefits.
- Explain the APN's role and partner benefits.

## Notes

### Marketplace basics

- A curated catalog of thousands of listings, mostly from independent software vendors (ISVs).
- It spans discovery, trial, purchase, deployment and management, with pricing and reviews per listing.

### Why use it

- Reusing existing software cuts total cost of ownership and speeds innovation.
- Buying is simpler, deploying and scaling are quick, and charges join your AWS bill.
- Pricing can be free, pay-as-you-go or annual.
- Compliance-ready software is quicker than an in-house build or working from documentation.

### What you can buy

- Software for security, networking, storage, ML and more, plus data and services.
- SaaS: business (project management), marketing (customer engagement) and collaboration (file sharing) apps.
- ML and AI: prebuilt models (image recognition, natural language processing) and training algorithms.
- Data and analytics: datasets, BI and data integration tools.
- Industry views, like healthcare tools for patient records or risk prediction.

### AWS Partner Network

- A global community of tech and consulting firms building customer solutions with AWS, solving problems and adding value.
- Customers can hire partners, like a retailer adding ML personalization.
- Partner benefits:
  - Funding, via partner programs, to build, market and sell with AWS.
  - Events (webinars, workshops, in person) for networking, updates and AWS experts.
  - Partner-centered content within the wider AWS Training and Certification, from certification to one service or goal.

## Key takeaways

- Marketplace sells ready third-party software, data and services.
- The APN helps partners build, market and sell AWS solutions.
- Funding is a key partner benefit.

## Scenarios

- Healthcare needs: browse by industry.
- Retail personalization: an ML partner.

## Terms

- **AWS Marketplace**: A curated catalog of third-party software, data and services.
- **Independent software vendor (ISV)**: A third-party seller on Marketplace.
- **Software as a service (SaaS)**: Marketplace's business, marketing and collaboration apps.
- **AWS Partner Network (APN)**: A global community building AWS solutions.
