# Introduction to Pricing and Support

## Objectives

- Explain that AWS offers both pricing and support options.

## Notes

### Scope

- The module asks what AWS costs a business and what help exists when you need it.
- Pricing: core concepts, plus the billing dashboard, AWS Budgets and AWS Cost Explorer.
- Support: support plans, AWS Marketplace and the AWS Partner Network.
- Cost optimization: a practical example.

### Coffee shop parallels

- Bills: the shop tracks monthly bills, as you track AWS spend.
- Support: a broken espresso machine or a lease question sends the owner to the provider's resources, as AWS customers turn to support.
- Efficiency: the shop trims waste, as AWS customers optimize cost.

## Visuals

- **Three-column overview**: Pricing, Support and Cost Optimization images previewing the sections.

## Key takeaways

- Billing tools help track, budget and forecast spending.
- Support spans plans, AWS Marketplace and partners.
- Cost optimization cuts waste and raises efficiency.

## Terms

- **Cost optimization**: Cutting waste and improving efficiency in the cloud.
